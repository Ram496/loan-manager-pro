📱 Loan Manager Pro

OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY & SECURITY DISCLOSURE

🔒 100% OFFLINE & AES-256 ENCRYPTED

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

Loan Manager Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing a completely private, secure, and transparent personal financial tracking application. This Privacy Policy details our strict zero-data collection commitment.

1. 🌐 Zero Remote Server & Zero Data Collection

Loan Manager Pro is built as a 100% offline application. We do not host remote servers, maintain databases, or collect any personal or financial data.

  • No Account Registration: You do not need an email, phone number, or password to use the app.
  • No Cloud Transmissions: All borrower names, lending balances, interest rates, and loan histories remain strictly stored on your physical device.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not integrate third-party analytics, ad trackers, or data miners.

2. 🔐 Hardware-Backed AES-256 Encryption

Your financial information is encrypted directly on your device using AndroidKeyStore hardware-backed cryptographic keys (AES-256 GCM encryption). Even if an unauthorized party gains access to your phone's file system, the database file (encrypted_loans_v2.bin) cannot be decrypted without your device's unique hardware key.

3. 🔑 App Passcode & Biometric Phone Lock

Loan Manager Pro offers optional biometrics (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) or a custom 4-digit App Passcode. Biometric authentication is handled locally by Android's native BiometricPrompt API. Biometric data is never accessed or stored by our app.

4. 📄 PDF Statements & Local Backups

All exported PDF statements and JSON database backup files are generated locally on your device. You maintain 100% control over where and with whom you share these files.

5. 🗑️ Data Erasure & Uninstallation

Since all data resides on your physical device, uninstalling the application or clearing the application data in Android System Settings permanently and unrecoverably erases all saved loan records and settings.

6. 📞 Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or app security, please reach out through our official GitHub repository page.
