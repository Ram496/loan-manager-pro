OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

🔒 100% OFFLINE & SECURE

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

Welcome to Loan Manager Pro. We value your privacy and are committed to keeping your financial records completely private, safe, and stored strictly on your device.

1. 🌐 100% Offline App (No Data Collection)

Loan Manager Pro works entirely offline. We do not operate cloud servers, collect personal details, or track user activity.

  • No Accounts Needed: You do not need an email, phone number, or login to use this app.
  • No Cloud Sync: All borrower details, loans, interest rates, and amounts stay stored only on your phone.
  • No Ad Tracking: We do not include third-party trackers, analytics, or advertising networks.

2. 🛡️ Secure Local Data Storage

All your saved records remain safely stored on your device's internal private storage. Your financial entries never leave your phone unless you choose to export them yourself.

3. 🔑 Optional App Security

You can protect app access using your phone's native lock screen (Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or PIN) or a custom 4-digit App Passcode. Authentication is handled locally by Android on your phone.

4. 📄 PDF Statements & Backups

Generated PDF reports and JSON backup files are created directly on your device. You have full control over saving, sharing, or deleting these files.

5. 🗑️ Data Removal

Because all information is kept only on your device, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your saved records.

6. 📞 Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official GitHub repository page.
